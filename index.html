About Me

Hello, I am an Earth Observation Scientist and Research Engineer currently working at GMV NSL, UK. I hold a Ph.D. in Physical Geography from East China Normal University, where I graduated in 2020. With over 3.5 years of post-doctoral research experience across China and the UK, I specialise in Earth Science research and the application of geoinformatics in urban planning and land use change analysis.

Currently, I am contributing to multiple European Space Agency (ESA) funded projects at GMV, focusing on the intersection of Earth Observation (EO) with public health, shipping emissions, and solar power generation. My research explores the impact of coastal land reclamation and its implications for climate change adaptation and urban resilience, particularly along Southeast Asian coasts. I have extensive experience using both optical and radar remote sensing techniques to map long-term coastal changes, including land use, land subsidence, vegetation, and surface water.

In addition to my academic research, I actively participate in international collaborations. I serve as a steering committee member of the International Geographical Union's Commission on Coastal Systems and am a Fellow of Future Earth Coasts. I am also a UK-JAPAN RENKEI fellow and part of the management team for GEO AquaWatch. My role involves integrating coastal zone management into Marine Spatial Planning (MSP) and policy, promoting coastal geoscience education, and building strategic scientific partnerships globally.
